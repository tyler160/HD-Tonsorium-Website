About

About HD Tonsorium

At HD Tonsorium Barber Shop, we're focused on one thing: helping you leave the shop looking and feeling your best.

From clean fades to classic cuts, our barbers take pride in delivering sharp results and friendly service. We work with a range of hair types and textures, and we keep the shop clean and comfortable from the moment you walk in.

Walk-ins are always welcome, and plenty of our regulars started that way — a first cut on a slow afternoon that turned into a standing appointment. We're proud to be part of the Katy community, right here on N Fry Road at Eagle Ranch Shopping Center.
